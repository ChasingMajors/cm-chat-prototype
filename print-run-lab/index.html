03 Establish production

Leave pack totals blank to solve from anchors, or enter verified totals. Each column represents packs of that format, not boxes or cases.

Default model: each format's pack total is the median of its numbered anchors' implied packs (eligible subjects × serial × odds); each parallel estimate = packs ÷ odds ÷ eligible subjects. Enter a pack total to override. The two options below are experimental and off by default; turn them on only to compare against the default.

Starting weights (experimental modes only): base and ordinary inserts 10; autographs and memorabilia 1. Change any weight below, or use 0 to exclude it. A weight is relative influence, not a confidence percentage. Estimates remain visible when anchors disagree.

Anchor checks

Each check compares checklist-based copies with the weighted production estimate. Autograph/memorabilia mismatches can reflect different eligible subjects in each parallel. Differences over 5% are flagged; automatic weighting reduces influence beyond 20% disagreement without changing the source odds. Blank weights use the displayed default.
